Frenchbrew Bookkeeper

Internal accounting and HR tool of Frenchbrew Cafe · Tanguile 2, Baloganon, Masinloc, Zambales, Philippines

Frenchbrew Bookkeeper is a private tool used only by the owner of Frenchbrew Cafe to run the cafe's own bookkeeping and payroll. It is not a public service and has no sign-up; only the cafe owner's Google account uses it.

What it does

  • Reads the cafe's own Google Sheets (daily sales reports, purchases, attendance, employee records) and writes results back to them.
  • Computes payroll, government contributions (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG) and withholding tax.
  • Prepares profit & loss, food cost and supplier payment reports, and emails them to the owner.
  • Saves supplier invoices received by email into the cafe's Google Drive.
