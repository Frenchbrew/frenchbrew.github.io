Privacy Policy

Frenchbrew Bookkeeper · Effective September 29, 2026

Frenchbrew Bookkeeper ("the app") is an internal tool of Frenchbrew Cafe, owned and operated by Louis F. Estioco, Tanguile 2, Baloganon, Masinloc, Zambales, Philippines. It is used only by the cafe owner, with the owner's own Google account, to run the cafe's bookkeeping, payroll and HR records.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Google Sheets and Google Drive: to read and update the cafe's own spreadsheets and documents (sales reports, purchases, inventory, attendance, employee records, payroll) and to save reports and supplier invoices in the cafe's Drive folders.
  • Gmail (read): to find supplier invoices and bills sent to the cafe's email and save their attachments to the cafe's Drive. Other emails are not stored or used.
  • Gmail (send): to send the cafe's weekly and monthly business reports to the owner and, only after the owner approves payroll, each employee's own payslip to that employee.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide the functions above for Frenchbrew Cafe. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with anyone except as described here.
  • Data stays in the cafe owner's Google account (Sheets, Drive, Gmail). Temporary working copies on the owner's computer or the owner's private automation account are deleted after processing.
  • Images of supplier invoices and cashier receipts may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to read the printed amounts. Anthropic processes them as a service provider and does not use API data to train its models.
  • Access credentials are stored privately by the owner and never published.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Employee personal data

Employee records (contact details, government numbers, pay) are processed as required by Philippine labor, tax and social security laws and the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), with each employee's consent where required. Employees may ask the cafe owner to see or correct their data.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions or requests: oneteracompany@gmail.com.
